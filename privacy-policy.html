Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Relic Rush Keno, an Android game developed by NavyB Technology, accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use the app.

1. Information Relic Rush Keno handles

Google sign-in and account information

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose to sign in, Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication may provide information used to authenticate you and create or identify your Relic Rush Keno player account. This can include a unique account identifier, display name, profile image, email address and authentication-related information.

Relic Rush Keno does not ask you to provide your Google password to the app.

Gameplay, progression and profile data

Relic Rush Keno stores information needed to preserve your game progress and profile. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • virtual credits, XP, level and total rounds played;
  • best win, total winnings and recent round history;
  • selected expedition and expedition/challenge progress;
  • Daily Cache and Daily Expedition claim/status information;
  • career/statistical counters and achievement progress;
  • unlocked achievements and selected Relic Avatar; and
  • profile update timestamps and related synchronization information.

Guest progress is stored locally on the Android device. When you sign in and use cloud backup, account-linked progress is also stored through Firebase Realtime Database so it can be synchronized and restored.

Advertising and device information

Relic Rush Keno uses Appodeal, an advertising mediation platform. The app may display an interstitial advertisement after a number of gameplay rounds. It may also offer optional rewarded advertisements when an eligible player claims the Daily Cache or Daily Expedition XP; the associated in-game reward is granted only after the advertising provider confirms completion.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices and the enabled advertising provider, Appodeal and its mediation partners may process data such as IP address, mobile advertising or other device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, operating-system information and other technical data used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, maintain security and improve ad services.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable law, your consent choices, device privacy settings and the configuration of the advertising services.

2. How information is used

Information handled by Relic Rush Keno is used for purposes including:

  • authenticating players who choose Google sign-in;
  • saving, synchronizing and restoring player progress;
  • maintaining game state, achievements, avatars and progression;
  • providing Daily Cache and Daily Expedition reward flows;
  • displaying and measuring advertising;
  • maintaining security and preventing abuse; and
  • diagnosing technical problems and improving app reliability.

3. Third-party services

Relic Rush Keno relies on third-party services that may process data under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication — optional player authentication;
  • Firebase Realtime Database — signed-in player progress and cloud backup; and
  • Appodeal and enabled mediation partners — interstitial and rewarded advertising, mediation, ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and security.

Google's privacy information for Google Sign-In and Firebase is available at Google Privacy Policy. Appodeal's privacy information, including information about its advertising services and partners, is available at the Appodeal Privacy Policy.

NavyB Technology does not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising choices and consent

Where required, the app or Appodeal may present a consent or privacy-options form before processing data for eligible advertising purposes. When privacy options are required for your region, Relic Rush Keno also provides a Privacy Settings entry in the app menu so you can review or change available choices. You may also manage eligible advertising and device-identifier choices through Android's privacy settings. Available controls vary by region, device and applicable law.

5. Local storage, cloud storage and security

Relic Rush Keno uses local Android app storage to preserve guest progress, signed-in cached progress and game/audio preferences. Signed-in cloud progress is stored using Firebase Realtime Database.

Online account and progress information is transmitted using encrypted network connections to Google/Firebase services. NavyB Technology uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the app. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Google, Appodeal and their respective service providers or advertising partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their applicable safeguards and privacy terms.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored only on your device may remain until you clear Relic Rush Keno's app data, reset the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Account-linked cloud progress may be retained while your Relic Rush Keno account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide cloud backup, maintain security, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your Relic Rush Keno account and associated account-linked data. After a valid deletion request is verified and processed, NavyB Technology will delete account-linked Relic Rush Keno data under its control, except where retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or other legal purposes.
